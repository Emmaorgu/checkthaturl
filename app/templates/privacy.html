Privacy

Last updated: 9 August 2025

No Storage

We don’t save the URLs you scan. Results are computed in-memory and discarded.

No Tracking

No cookies. No ads. No analytics beacons following you around.

Security‑first

We fetch pages safely, sanitize inputs, and minimize data in transit.

What we process (and why)

URL you submit: used to fetch the page and run phishing checks (domain, content, link structure, and behavioral signals if enabled).

Page content (transient): fetched to compute features (e.g., suspicious keywords, timers, anchor mismatches). We do not store full page content.

Derived features (transient): numeric signals our model uses to produce a verdict and explanation. Discarded after response.

What we don’t collect

  • No accounts, emails, names, or identifiers
  • No cookies for tracking or advertising
  • No persistent logs of submitted URLs or results

How scanning works

We analyze the URL and fetch the page to extract signals like domain age, TLD, keyword density, link/anchor mismatches, urgency timers, and more. Our model then assigns a probability and returns a verdict: Legitimate, Suspicious, or Phishing, with grouped reasons.

We do not use the page content for any purpose beyond this one‑time analysis.

Third‑party services

We do not sell data or use ad networks. Optional integrations (e.g., threat feeds like OpenPhish/URLhaus or Google Safe Browsing) are used to verify risk but are not given your identity.

Security

  • Input sanitization and safe HTTP clients
  • Minimal data retention (in‑memory only)
  • Feature‑level logging only when debugging (disabled in production)

Your choices

If you don’t want the app to fetch a live page, you can analyze a non‑sensitive placeholder or a test URL first. For sensitive internal links, run a self‑hosted instance behind your firewall.
