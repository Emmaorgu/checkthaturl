FAQ

Last updated: 9 August 2025

Do you store the URLs I scan?

No. We don’t store scanned URLs, set tracking cookies, or sell data. Results are computed in‑memory and discarded.

What does the “Suspicious” verdict mean?

It’s a caution band. We detected some risk signals, but not enough for a definitive “Phishing.” Treat it carefully and verify the source.

Which signals do you analyze?

Domain risks (age, TLD, WHOIS anomalies, entropy), content cues (keyword density, login prompts, urgency timers), and link/behavioral patterns (anchor mismatches, excessive externals, redirects).

Can I self‑host CheckThatURL?

Yes. For sensitive workflows, deploy the app within your network. Disable any optional external lookups and keep models/data local.

Do you check against threat feeds?

Optionally, yes (e.g., OpenPhish, URLhaus, Google Safe Browsing). Lookups are one‑way for verification; we don’t share your identity.

Will you ever add accounts or analytics?

Only if strictly necessary and privacy‑preserving (self‑hosted analytics, opt‑in). The default experience remains no‑login, no‑tracking.
